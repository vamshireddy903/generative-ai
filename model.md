model is a trained program that has learned patterns from data and can make predictions or generate outputs for new inputs.

Simple Definition

A model is the "brain" of an AI system that has been trained on data to perform a specific task.

Real-Life Analogy

Similarly:

**Training data** = Millions or billions of examples  
**Training process** = AI learns patterns  
**Model** = The learned knowledge  
**Prompt** = Your question  
**Response** = The model's answer  

# Example 1: Spam Detection

Training data:
```
Email: "Win a free iPhone"      → Spam
Email: "Meeting at 2 PM"        → Not Spam
Email: "Claim your prize"       → Spam
```
After learning these examples, the model can classify a new email as spam or not spam.

# Example 2: ChatGPT

During training, the model reads a huge amount of text.

After training, it becomes an LLM (Large Language Model).

You ask:

Explain Kubernetes.

The model uses what it learned to generate an answer.
